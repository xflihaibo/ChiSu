The DevLens logo was iteratively refined to focus on professional yet friendly aesthetics. The final design is circular, using the project's theme colors (Indigo #4F46E5 and Magenta #FF00FF). It visually combines a magnifying glass (for the Inspector/Picker) and a ruler (for measurement), where the ruler marks are integrated into the magnifying glass's handle. This creates a cohesive "all-in-one" tool feeling.
