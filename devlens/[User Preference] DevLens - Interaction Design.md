# [User Preference] DevLens - Interaction Design

## UI/UX Preferences
- **Mode Switching**: Prefers explicit mode switching (Ruler, Picker, Inspector) rather than complex combined shortcuts to avoid tool conflict.
- **Immersive Mode**: Preferred a full-screen overlay mode that hides/freezes page interaction.
- **Color Picking**: Magnifying glass (Loupe) with click-to-copy behavior.
- **Inspector**: Click to lock a detail card instead of just hovering.
- **Ruler**: Automatic distance marking between lines.
- **Box Model**: Automatic highlighting of padding and margin upon interaction.
